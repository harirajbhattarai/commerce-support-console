## Section D — Troubleshooting

### D1. Not Turning On

**Customer questions this covers**:
- "won't turn on", "not turning on", "does nothing when I press the button", "dead", "no response", "lights not coming on"

**Answer**:

"If your hoverboard will not turn on, try these steps: 1. Confirm the battery is charged — connect the charger and check the LED turns red (charging). Leave it on charge for at least 20 minutes before trying again. 2. Press and hold the power button firmly for 3–5 full seconds. 3. Inspect the charging port for debris or bent pins. 4. If the board has been stored unused for a long time, the battery may have fully drained and may need a longer charge before it responds. If it still does not turn on after a full charge attempt, contact contact@hoverboardstore.co.uk with your order number, a description of the issue, and whether the charger LED lights up when connected."

### D2. Charger LED Not Coming On

**Customer questions this covers**:
- "charger light not working", "no light on charger", "charger not lighting up", "charger not working"

**Answer**:

"If the charger LED is not illuminating: 1. Check that the charger is firmly plugged into the wall socket and that the socket is switched on. 2. Ensure the charger connector is fully seated into the hoverboard charging port. 3. Inspect the charging port on the hoverboard for bent pins, dust, or debris. 4. Try the charger in a different wall socket. If the LED still does not light up after all checks, the charger may be faulty. Do not use a replacement charger unless it is an approved Hoverboard Store replacement. Contact contact@hoverboardstore.co.uk with your order number."

### D3. Charger LED Colour Meanings

**Customer questions this covers**:
- "what does the red light mean", "charger is green not charging", "what do the colours mean on the charger"

**Answer**:

"Charger LED colours: Red = charging in progress. Green = fully charged. No light = check connection. If the charger light is green from the moment you plug it in (without a red charging phase), the battery may already be full, or there may be a battery fault. If the charger becomes very hot to the touch, or if it shows no light after all connections are confirmed, stop using it and contact contact@hoverboardstore.co.uk."

### D4. Beeping / Flashing Lights

**Customer questions this covers**:
- "hoverboard is beeping", "lights are flashing", "making a noise", "beeping constantly", "red light flashing and beeping"

**Answer**:

"Beeping and flashing lights usually mean the hoverboard needs calibration or the battery is low. First, check the battery level — if it is low, charge the board fully before attempting calibration. To calibrate and reset: 1. Place the hoverboard on a completely flat, level surface. 2. Ensure the board is powered off. 3. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds until the lights flash rapidly. 4. Release the button. 5. Turn the board off. 6. Turn it back on. The board should now be calibrated. If beeping continues after a full charge and calibration, contact contact@hoverboardstore.co.uk with your order number."

### D5. Balance Issue / Tilting to One Side

**Customer questions this covers**:
- "it tilts to one side", "not balanced", "one side goes down", "hard to balance", "keeps tipping"

**Answer**:

"If the hoverboard tilts consistently to one side during use, calibration is usually the fix. Perform a calibration reset: turn the board off, place it on a completely flat surface, hold the power button for 10 seconds until the lights flash, release, turn off, then turn on again. If the tilt persists after calibration, ensure the surface you are riding on is flat. If the tilt is severe or the board veers strongly to one side during riding, this may indicate a wheel motor or sensor fault — contact contact@hoverboardstore.co.uk with your order number and a description of the behaviour."
